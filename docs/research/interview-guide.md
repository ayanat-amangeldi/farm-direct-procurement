## Farmer Questions — F1 and F2

1. How do you currently find buyers and receive orders?
2. What information do buyers usually ask for before placing an order?
3. How do you set and update your prices?
4. How do you manage available quantities and minimum orders?
5. What problems occur when confirming, preparing, or cancelling orders?

## Buyer Questions — B1 and B2

1. Tell us about your most recent purchase of agricultural products.
2. How did you find and compare sellers?
3. Besides price, what influenced your choice?
4. Were delivery costs, minimum quantities, and the final total clear before you ordered?
5. How did you receive confirmation and updates about your order?

## Use of the Evidence

The [evidence summary](../evidence-of-relevance.md) groups the reported responses
and maps findings to requirements. Requirements are the team's interpretation
of participants' reported procurement experiences. These interviews establish
initial user needs; they are not a prototype usability evaluation or evidence of
measured improvements in cost, task time or farmer income. The small sample
limits generalisation.
