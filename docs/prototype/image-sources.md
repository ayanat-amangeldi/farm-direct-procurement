# Product Photography

Six illustrative images were generated with the built-in ImageGen tool on
23 October 2026 for the Flutter prototype. They depict generic produce, not
actual listed inventory or actual farms. Product prices and inventory remain
fictional fixtures. No reference images were used.

## Saved Assets

- `apps/mobile/assets/products/potatoes.png`
- `apps/mobile/assets/products/carrots.png`
- `apps/mobile/assets/products/tomatoes.png`
- `apps/mobile/assets/products/cucumbers.png`
- `apps/mobile/assets/products/apples.png`
- `apps/mobile/assets/products/wheat.png`

## Prompt Set

Each asset was generated in a separate built-in tool call using the following
prompt, substituting the subject from the list below for SUBJECT:

Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: agricultural marketplace product card
photograph. Subject: SUBJECT. Photorealistic premium food catalog photography,
close-up, softly lit warm neutral stone surface, natural diffused daylight,
realistic colour and texture, restrained professional editorial style. Product
centered, fills about 70 percent of square frame, all main produce contained
with breathing room. Consistent soft warm gray-beige backdrop and photographic
treatment across the catalog. No people, no packaging, no text, no logos, no
border, no collage, no illustration, no cartoon, no plastic 3D look. Square
composition.

Subjects:

- potatoes: A small pile of freshly harvested golden-brown potatoes with natural skin texture
- carrots: A small neat bunch of fresh orange carrots with green tops
- tomatoes: A small cluster of ripe red tomatoes with a few natural green stems
- cucumbers: Several fresh dark green cucumbers with natural textured skin
- apples: A small group of fresh red and green apples
- wheat: A mound of golden wheat grain with a few wheat ears beside it
