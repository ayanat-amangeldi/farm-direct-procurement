# System Features

## 1. Farmer Features
•⁠  ⁠Farmer registration and authentication
•⁠  ⁠Create and manage product listings
•⁠  ⁠Set and update product prices
•⁠  ⁠View and manage customer orders

•⁠  ⁠Progress orders through readiness and completion, or reject them with a reason

## 2. Buyer Features
•⁠  ⁠Buyer registration and authentication
•⁠  ⁠Browse agricultural products
•⁠  ⁠Search and filter products
•⁠  ⁠Compare product prices
•⁠  ⁠Place and track orders

## 3. Transparent Pricing
•⁠  ⁠Display clear product prices
•⁠  ⁠Show price breakdowns
•⁠  ⁠Provide information about farmers
•⁠  ⁠Display delivery costs separately

•⁠  ⁠Show dated unit-price and delivery-fee change history

## 4. Administration
•⁠  ⁠Manage user accounts
•⁠  ⁠Moderate product listings
•⁠  ⁠Monitor orders and transactions

## Prototype implementation status

Local buyer/farmer registration, sign-in and role-specific workspaces are
implemented. Each farmer owns one farm; buyers see their own orders. Seeded demo
administrators have read-only account, offer and order oversight. Production
authentication, account management, listing moderation and transaction monitoring
still require backend implementation.
